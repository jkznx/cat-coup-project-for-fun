# Multiplayer tabletop

## Direction contract
THESIS: Playful deception at a cozy midnight cat lounge; the table and the current decision lead.
OWN-WORLD: Midnight blue felt, amber-gold actions, warm pale text, green secondary controls, expressive original cats. Geist gives small controls clear forms.
STORY: Enter a name, create or join a room, ready up, then protect private influence while reading public claims.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand and rules in a compact top bar. A large cat-card fan beside the room form. In play, opponents sit above a central decision panel, with the player's hand and action tray below. Mobile stacks seats above decisions and hand.
FORM: Midnight Cat Lounge, assigned grounded candidate 6, seed c083ca67. User confirmed in chat after the decision-page process failed twice. Code-first confirmed. The alternatives considered were royal court, cafe menu, comic panels, game-box inserts, cat-club badges, midnight lounge and theater playbill. Catalog challengers offered weaker game clarity; retain distinct state marking and deliberate motion as quality lessons.
SIGNATURE: Cards fan into place on arrival; new decisions settle into view. GSAP animations honor prefers-reduced-motion and never gate actions. The operative game does not use pinned scrolling.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
